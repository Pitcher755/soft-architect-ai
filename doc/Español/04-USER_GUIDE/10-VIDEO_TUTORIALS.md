## 📹 How to Contribute Videos

Have an interesting use case? **Share it with the community.**

### Contribution Process

1. **Record your video** (format: MP4, 1080p, 30fps)
2. **Upload to YouTube** (public or unlisted)
3. **Open issue on GitHub:** https://github.com/Pitcher755/soft-architect-ai/issues
   - Title: `[VIDEO] Use case name`
   - Descripción: Video link + summary
   - Tag: `documentoation`, `video-tutorial`
4. **Team review** (2-3 days)
5. **If approved:** Added to this page and official channel

---

### Suggested Video Template

```markdown
**Intro (30s):**
- Hi, I'm [name] and today I'll show you [topic]

**Problem (2 min):**
- Project context
- Specific challenges

**Solution with SoftArchitect AI (10-20 min):**
- Live demonstration
- Decision explanation
- Tips and tricks

**Results (2 min):**
- What was achieved
- Metrics (time saved, quality, etc.)

**Conclusion (1 min):**
- Lessons learned
- Call to action (GitHub, Discord)
```

---

| Tool | Type | Price | Platform |

### Technical Requirements

- ✅ Resolution: 1920x1080 (Full HD)
- ✅ Framerate: 30fps (minimum)
- ✅ Audio: Decent microphone (avoid background noise)
- ✅ Language: Spanish or English (optional subtitles)
- ✅ Duration: 10-45 minutes (ideal: 15-25 min)
